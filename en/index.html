Situations she can help with

Foreign businesses usually reach a Chinese lawyer with one of these problems. Each note gives the main Chinese rule and the related work she has done.

  • Your Chinese JV partner is shutting you out

    Meetings held without you, decisions you never voted on, financial records withheld. A shareholder can demand to inspect the accounting books and the vouchers behind them (invoices, contracts, bank records) and sue if refused; challenge resolutions passed in breach of procedure, generally within 60 days; and, after a written demand to the board or supervisors, sue managers on the company's behalf (a derivative action). A deadlocked company can be dissolved by court order.

    PRC Company Law (revised 2023) Arts. 26, 57, 189 and 231

    Related work (shareholder cases in China): won a court ruling extending a shareholder's inspection right to accounting vouchers; acted for a minority shareholder seeking judicial dissolution of a deadlocked company.

  • You resigned, but you are still the legal representative

    Foreign general managers often stay on the register as a Chinese company's legal representative after leaving. If the company has unpaid debts, courts can then restrict that person's high spending and travel. The company must appoint a replacement within 30 days of the resignation; if it won't, a court can order the registration removed.

    PRC Company Law (revised 2023) Art. 10

    Related work: obtained a court ruling removing a departed executive from a company's legal-representative registration.

  • Your shares are held in someone else's name

    Some foreign investors hold shares in a Chinese company through a Chinese nominee. The nominee agreement binds the two of you; to be registered as a shareholder you generally need the consent of more than half of the other shareholders, or proof that they knew of your investment and did not object. For a foreign investor, registration also has foreign-investment consequences that need checking first.

    Supreme People's Court Company Law Interpretation III, Art. 24

    Related work: acted for a shareholder whose shares were held through a nominee arrangement; has written on how such shareholders become registered.

  • A Chinese supplier or customer is in breach

    Defective goods, unpaid invoices, guarantees called beyond their terms. She reviews the contract and its dispute-resolution clause, assesses liability and the realistic route to recovery, and acts in litigation or arbitration in China.

    Related work: led the handling of an international trade contract dispute with a Middle East counterparty; reduced a guarantor's liability for amounts beyond the agreed scope.

  • You are doing a share deal with a Chinese party

    Share purchase and investment agreements in Chinese and English, where the two versions must say the same thing; investor rights such as pre-emption, anti-dilution, redemption and liquidation preference.

    Related work: reviewed the bilingual agreements in a China–Italy equity transfer; identified conflicting terms across a technology company's financing rounds and proposed how to reconcile them.

  • You are setting up or running a company in China

    Setting up a wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) or a joint venture; articles of association and governance that work under the PRC Company Law; older joint ventures that still treat the board as the highest authority.

    Foreign Investment Law; PRC Company Law (revised 2023)

  • You need to hire, discipline or dismiss staff in China

    Terminations and severance, non-compete and confidentiality obligations, employee handbooks, labor dispatch and outsourcing, work-injury claims. An unlawful dismissal costs twice the statutory severance.

    PRC Labor Contract Law Arts. 47 and 87

Working with a Chinese lawyer from abroad

Practical points foreign clients usually ask about early on.

  • LanguageChinese courts and arbitration commissions work in Chinese, and foreign-language evidence needs a Chinese translation. You can work with Ms. Cheng in English throughout; she explains Chinese filings and judgments to you in English.
  • Documents from homeSince 7 November 2023 China applies the Apostille Convention. Public documents from Italy, the United States and other contracting states can be authenticated with an apostille instead of consular legalisation; private documents usually need notarising first.
  • Two-language contractsCheck which version prevails. If both are equally authentic and they differ, Chinese law reads the wording in light of the contract's purpose and good faith (PRC Civil Code Art. 466), so reconciling the versions before signing is cheaper than arguing about them later.
  • Who can appear in courtA foreign party that wants a lawyer in Chinese court proceedings must instruct a PRC lawyer. Ms. Cheng can coordinate with your counsel at home on questions of foreign law.
  • Remote workingMost work can be done by email and video call, with meetings in Shanghai when needed. Shanghai is 7 hours ahead of Italy in winter (6 in summer) and 12–13 hours ahead of New York.

Frequently asked questions

General information on Chinese law, not legal advice on your situation. Last reviewed October 2026.

Can she represent a foreign company in Chinese courts?

Yes. Yijing Cheng is a PRC-licensed lawyer. Foreign companies and individuals who want a lawyer to act for them in Chinese court proceedings must instruct a PRC lawyer, and she can also act in arbitration seated in China. She works with clients in English.

My Chinese partner holds meetings without me. Are those decisions valid?

Not necessarily. Under the PRC Company Law (revised 2023), a resolution is not established if, for example, no meeting was actually held or the required attendance or votes were not reached (Art. 27). If the meeting was called or the vote taken in breach of the law or the articles of association, a shareholder can ask the court to revoke the resolution within 60 days of it being made; a shareholder who was not notified has 60 days from learning of it, and the right lapses one year after the resolution (Art. 26). Act quickly and keep every notice and email.

Can a minority shareholder in a Chinese company see the accounting records?

Generally yes. Under Article 57 of the PRC Company Law (revised 2023, in force since 1 July 2024), a shareholder may request access to the accounting books and accounting vouchers by a written request stating the purpose. The company may refuse only with reasonable grounds to believe the purpose is improper, and must reply in writing within 15 days. If it refuses, the shareholder can sue, and may use accountants or lawyers to carry out the inspection.

Our joint venture was set up under the old JV law. Do we need to change anything?

The Foreign Investment Law gave older joint ventures five years, to the end of 2024, to bring their organisational form and governance in line with the PRC Company Law. Under the Company Law the shareholders' meeting, not the board, is the highest authority, so articles and JV contracts drafted on the old model should be reviewed.

I resigned as general manager but I'm still the legal representative. What can I do?

Under Article 10 of the PRC Company Law, a director or manager serving as legal representative who resigns is deemed to resign as legal representative too, and the company must appoint a replacement within 30 days. If it does not update the registration, a court action can be brought to remove it. Restrictions linked to the company's debts are dealt with separately in the enforcement proceedings.

Can documents from Italy or the US be used in Chinese courts?

Yes, with the right formalities. Public documents from Apostille Convention states such as Italy and the US can be apostilled instead of legalised by a Chinese consulate (since 7 November 2023); private documents usually need notarising first, and a Chinese translation must be attached.

Our contract's Chinese and English versions differ. Which applies?

First check whether the contract says which language prevails. If both versions are equally authentic, the wording is presumed to mean the same; where it differs, it is interpreted in light of the relevant clauses, the nature and purpose of the contract and good faith (PRC Civil Code Art. 466).

How does dismissal of an employee work in China?

Employers can terminate only on grounds set out in the PRC Labor Contract Law. Statutory severance is generally one month's wages per full year of service (Art. 47); an unlawful termination costs twice that amount (Art. 87). Most employment disputes must go to labor arbitration before court, and the limitation period is generally one year.
