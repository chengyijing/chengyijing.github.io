关于我About Me

程轶婧律师是上海市君悦律师事务所律师，君悦律师事务所证券与上市委员会委员，中美涉外律师。 Yijing Cheng is an attorney at MHP Law Firm (Shanghai Junyue Law Offices), a member of the firm's Securities & Capital Markets Committee, and a China-US cross-border lawyer working in both Chinese and English.

程律师专注公司法与股权纠纷、劳动争议及商事争议解决，擅长处理股东资格确认、股权代持显名、股东知情权纠纷、股东代表诉讼、公司解散与清算、法定代表人涤除登记等公司治理与股东争议案件，以及劳动合同纠纷、工伤认定与赔偿、公司内部劳务合规等用工争议。 Ms. Cheng focuses on corporate law, equity disputes, labor disputes and commercial dispute resolution. She is experienced in shareholder status confirmation, nominee shareholding disclosure, shareholder information rights, shareholder derivative actions, company dissolution and liquidation, and removal of legal representatives, as well as labor contract disputes, work-related injury compensation and internal labor compliance.

程律师本科毕业于上海财经大学经济法专业，获上海财经大学法学院优秀毕业生称号；后赴美于范德堡大学法学院（Vanderbilt University Law School）取得LL.M.法学硕士学位，并获范德堡大学法学院奖学金（Merit-Based Scholarship）。凭借中美两地法律教育背景与实务经验，程律师熟悉中美两地商业与法律环境，入选上海国际法律服务枢纽涉外人才名录。 Ms. Cheng earned her LL.B. in Economic Law from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics, where she was honored as an Outstanding Graduate of the Law School. She then received her LL.M. from Vanderbilt University Law School in the United States, awarded with a Merit-Based Scholarship. With legal education and practical experience in both China and the US, she is familiar with the business and legal environments of both markets, and has been selected for the Shanghai International Legal Services Hub Foreign-Related Talent List.

2023 年 12 月起从事法律工作，先后在上海明伦律师事务所实习、任实习律师、执业律师，并曾担任原执业律所北美办公室负责人；2026 年 7 月起执业于上海市君悦律师事务所。 Ms. Cheng has been engaged in legal practice since December 2023. She interned and practiced at Shanghai Minglun Law Offices, where she also served as Head of the firm's North America Office, and has been practicing at MHP Law Firm since July 2026.

程律师创办并运营法律专业公众号「程律视界-Law&Beyond」，长期输出公司法、股权纠纷与劳动法实务文章，并受邀参与公司治理、跨境投资等主题研讨会进行专业分享。 She founded and runs a legal WeChat blog "Law&Beyond", regularly publishing articles on corporate law, equity disputes and labor law, and is a frequent speaker at seminars on corporate governance and cross-border investment.

2026 年 9 月，参加上海市律师协会与香港德辅大律师事务所合作举办的国际仲裁培训。 In September 2026, she completed an international arbitration training program jointly held by the Shanghai Bar Association and Des Voeux Chambers (Hong Kong).

公司治理 · 诉讼Corporate Governance · Litigation

代理离职高管诉请涤除法定代表人登记Removing a Departed Executive from Legal Representative Registration

委托人辞任后仍被登记为法定代表人，因公司僵局无法通过内部程序办理变更，持续面临限高、被执行等法律风险。围绕"委托关系终止后公司负有办理变更登记义务"这一核心争点，依据《公司法》相关规定论证委托法律关系已告解除的法律后果，并梳理公司怠于履行义务的证据链条，最终获得法院支持，涤除其法定代表人登记。After resignation, the client remained registered as the company's legal representative and faced mounting enforcement risks. Arguing that the company is obligated to update the registration once the mandate has ended under the PRC Company Law, we built the evidence chain of the company's failure to act and secured a court ruling removing the client from the registration.

公司治理 · 诉讼Corporate Governance · Litigation

代理小股东诉请解散公司Dissolution Action Brought by a Minority Shareholder

公司长期陷入经营僵局，股东会无法形成有效决议，小股东投资目的落空。以"公司经营管理发生严重困难、继续存续将损害股东利益"为请求权基础，系统收集股东会长期无法召开、决策机制失灵、经营停滞等方面的证据，完成对公司僵局的要件化论证，推进公司解散之诉。With shareholder meetings unable to function and the company deadlocked, we built an element-by-element case for judicial dissolution on the ground that the company's operations were in serious difficulty and its continuance would harm shareholders' interests, systematically collecting evidence of failed meetings and stalled operations.

公司治理 · 诉讼Corporate Governance · Litigation

代理隐名股东诉请查阅公司会计账簿及凭证Shareholder Information Rights: Access to Accounting Vouchers

委托人作为隐名股东，就知情权行使范围与公司产生争议。围绕"股东知情权是否涵盖会计凭证"这一实践中的争议焦点，论证会计账簿与会计凭证的内在关联，主张查阅范围应扩展至原始凭证以保障知情权的实质实现，相关主张获得法院支持。Acting for a concealed shareholder, we argued that the right to inspect accounting books should extend to underlying accounting vouchers to give the information right real substance — a position accepted by the court.

投融资 · 非诉Investment & Financing · Non-Contentious

某科技公司多轮融资项目Multiple Financing Rounds for a Technology Company

针对企业历经多轮融资、投资条款层层叠加的复杂情况，系统梳理各轮次投资协议中的优先认购权、反稀释、回购、优先清算等核心条款，识别不同轮次条款之间的冲突与衔接问题；在此基础上协调投资方与创始团队利益诉求，提出条款优化与衔接方案，并修订公司章程相关条款，保障融资进程平稳推进。For a company after multiple financing rounds, we mapped core terms across instruments — preemptive rights, anti-dilution, redemption and liquidation preferences — identified conflicts between rounds, reconciled investor and founder interests, and amended the articles of association to keep the financing on track.

破产清算 · 非诉Bankruptcy · Non-Contentious

某制造企业破产清算案Bankruptcy Liquidation of a Manufacturing Enterprise

企业陷入债务危机、面临清算，需在保护债权人利益与资产处置之间寻求平衡。全面分析企业债务结构与资产负债状况，协助制定清算方案，并主导设计股权置换方案以化解债务、盘活资产；同步起草股权置换协议及配套法律文件，把控交易结构与法律风险。Facing a debt crisis, we analyzed the enterprise's debt structure, assisted in formulating the liquidation plan, and designed a share-swap scheme to resolve debts and revitalize assets, drafting the swap agreement and related documents while controlling transaction risks.

涉外 · 并购Cross-Border · M&A

某中意投资并购项目China-Italy Investment and Acquisition Project

跨境交易中中英文文本表述差异往往埋藏权利义务分歧。参与股权转让全程，审核中英文双语投资协议，重点协调优先购买权、知识产权归属、陈述与保证等条款在中英文语境下的表述差异，消除语义歧义，确保交易文件的准确性与可执行性。Participating throughout the equity transfer, we reviewed the bilingual (Chinese-English) investment agreements and reconciled discrepancies in rights of first refusal, IP ownership and representations & warranties between the two language versions, eliminating ambiguity to keep the documents accurate and enforceable.

涉外 · 争议解决Cross-Border · Dispute Resolution

某中东贸易合同纠纷Trade Contract Dispute with a Middle East Counterparty

主导处理国际贸易合同项下争议，围绕合同履行中的违约事实与责任认定展开工作，逐条梳理合同条款以明确双方权利义务边界，重点厘清违约责任承担方式及争议解决条款的适用，为客户厘清权利主张路径与应对策略。Leading the handling of an international trade contract dispute, we analyzed breach facts and liability clause by clause, clarified the parties' rights and obligations, and defined the liability allocation and dispute resolution path for the client.

涉外 · 公司设立Cross-Border · Company Formation

北美某前沿科技公司设立Formation of a Frontier Technology Company in North America

负责该前沿科技公司在北美的设立相关法律事务，统筹公司治理结构搭建、公司章程与设立文件的起草审查，并就跨境架构下的主体类型选择、合规要点提供法律支持，协助完成设立阶段的各项法律工作。We handled the legal aspects of establishing the company in North America, structuring its governance framework, drafting and reviewing the charter and formation documents, and advising on entity selection and compliance under a cross-border structure.

合同纠纷 · 诉讼Contract Dispute · Litigation

代理债权人主张选择履行原债务Creditor's Right to Elect Performance of the Original Obligation

债务人未履行以物抵债协议，就"新旧债务并存时债权人可否择一主张"这一争议焦点，援引《民法典合同编通则司法解释》相关规定，论证新债与旧债并存的清偿关系及债权人的选择履行请求权基础，为委托人厘清权利实现路径。Where the debtor failed to perform an agreement substituting payment in kind, we invoked the Judicial Interpretation on the Contract Part of the Civil Code to establish that old and new obligations coexist and the creditor may elect to claim performance of the original obligation.

担保纠纷 · 诉讼Guarantee Dispute · Litigation

代理保证人主张限定担保范围Limiting the Scope of a Guarantor's Liability

债权人主张的保证责任范围超出约定，围绕担保合同条款的解释与保证责任范围认定展开工作，逐项分析担保合同关于保证范围、保证期间及责任限额的约定，明确保证人仅就约定范围承担责任的边界，成功减免超出部分的赔偿责任。Against claims exceeding the agreed scope, we analyzed the guarantee contract's provisions on scope, period and liability caps, established the boundaries of the guarantor's obligations, and successfully reduced the liability beyond the agreed terms.
